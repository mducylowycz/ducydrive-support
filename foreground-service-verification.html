Current release information

Version 4.0.0 (276) was submitted to Google Play for review on November 7, 2026. The current production release is 3.0.0 (246). Check Google Play for the version available to your device.

Two-stage Easy setup, expanded formatting choices and free experimental Data rescue are included in the submitted 4.0 update. Premium actions require a one-time purchase. The current U.S. price is US$1.99; regional prices and applicable taxes vary. Compare access · 4.0 changes.
