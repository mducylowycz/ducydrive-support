Privacy Policy

Effective July 25, 2026 · Developer: Michael Ducylowycz

Overview

DucyDrive AIO USB Tools performs USB inspection, formatting, imaging, verification, benchmark, wipe, recovery, and diagnostic operations locally on the user’s Android device.

Data collection and sharing

The application does not collect, sell, transmit, or share USB file contents, filenames, device serial numbers, advertising identifiers, or Android identifiers. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or user-account SDK.

User-selected files

A user may explicitly select a file or destination through Android’s system document picker. The application accesses that item only to perform the requested local backup, image-writing, or report-export operation. The application does not upload these files.

Google Play Billing

The optional permanent Premium product is sold through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes the transaction and provides product, purchase-state, and purchase-token information needed to grant and restore access. DucyDrive does not receive or store payment-card details.

Information stored on the device

Operation state, privacy-safe results, preferences, and protected recovery metadata may be stored privately on the device. Private app information is removed when the user clears the app’s storage or uninstalls it. Exported files remain under the user’s control and can be deleted through the selected document provider.

Accounts and deletion

DucyDrive does not create or maintain user accounts. Users can remove locally stored app information by clearing application storage or uninstalling the application.

Changes

If these practices change, this policy and the corresponding Google Play Data Safety declaration will be updated before the changed release is distributed.

Contact

Privacy and support inquiries can be sent to mducylowycz@outlook.com or submitted through the DucyDrive support page.
