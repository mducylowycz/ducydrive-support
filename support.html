Support

Report a reproducible problem or ask a question about DucyDrive AIO USB Tools through the project’s public support tracker.

Include the Android version, phone model, USB device type, app version, operation selected, and the exact error message when available.

Do not post license files, purchase tokens, personal information, private documents, device identifiers, signing material, or confidential diagnostic data in a public report.

For private support or information that should not appear in a public issue, email mducylowycz@outlook.com.
