DucyDrive release history

What's new

A concise record of completed features included in public releases. Future versions will be added here when they ship.

July 30, 2026 · Direct Full Access release candidate

Version 2.2.0 RC 76

  • Adds an admitted, original x64 UEFI:NTFS driver and same-device chainloader to the Direct Full Access edition.
  • Builds large Windows installation media with a small FAT32 EFI System Partition and a larger NTFS payload partition.
  • Verifies GPT structures, filesystem metadata, EFI paths, packaged binary hashes, provenance, and read-back results.
  • Adds clearer beginner guidance, exact boot-result receipts, image inspection, and safer pre-write rejection.
  • Supports this UEFI:NTFS engineering path with Secure Boot turned off. Secure Boot and Legacy BIOS are not claimed for this build.

July 30, 2026

What the 2.2 program adds

  • Automatic ISO, IMG, raw DMG, Windows, Linux, rescue, and compressed-image inspection.
  • FAT32 split-WIM Windows media construction and transactional NTFS/ESP media creation.
  • Source hashing, byte read-back, partition, filesystem, and EFI loader-path validation.
  • Streaming gzip, ZIP, XZ, Bzip2, and Zstandard image support without a full temporary decompressed copy.
  • Detailed receipts that distinguish structural checks from QEMU or physical-firmware proof.

July 27, 2026

Version 2.0.1

  • Streams gzip-compressed raw images directly to USB.
  • Adds optional expected SHA-256 matching and read-back verification control.
  • Adds valid-GPT relocation and stale-metadata cleanup controls.
  • Expands the in-app User Manual across every major workflow.
  • Updates public support and privacy contact information.

July 27, 2026

Version 2.0.0

  • Introduces verified byte-for-byte IMG, ISO, and raw DMG restoration.
  • Reports detected ISO 9660, El Torito, MBR, GPT, and EFI structures.
  • Invalidates obsolete recovery snapshots before raw image writes.
  • Restricts USB transport to the supported Bulk-Only protocol.

Release policy

Only completed and released behavior appears here. Research, planned upgrades, and presentation-only concepts are excluded until they are executable and verified.
