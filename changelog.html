DucyDrive release history

What's new

Public release history and the current status of each update.

October 9, 2026 · Version 2.5.2 (185) · SUBMITTED FOR GOOGLE PLAY REVIEW

A searchable file explorer inside DucyDrive

This update is not yet available to customers. Its signed release build and refreshed phone and tablet screenshots have been submitted together for Play review. Preview the actual build screens.

  • Open an Android-approved local or mounted USB folder in the free copy workflow, then navigate subfolders, search the current folder, sort by name, size, or type, and select multiple files or folders.
  • Choose a destination through the same in-app browser while retaining the existing non-overwriting preview, duplicate-name choices, cancellation, and file verification.
  • Keep browsing read-only. Android's folder picker controls access; the app does not obtain unrestricted storage access or upload file contents.

A disposable virtual-device copy passed; physical USB behavior and a real Play purchase remain unverified.

October 8, 2026 · Version 2.5.1 (184) · AVAILABLE ON GOOGLE PLAY

A warmer look and more useful formatting guidance

  • A coordinated cream, amber, gold and dark-brown interface keeps the original duck, improves contrast, and adds magnifying-glass cues to search fields.
  • Target-device guidance for TV, camera, car stereo, computers and boot media explains recommended formats, layouts, file-size limits and why. Exact model compatibility remains unverified.
  • A planned single-file-size check catches FAT/FAT32 limits before erase; a capacity-aware SD/microSD setup handles small, SDHC-size and SDXC-size cards conservatively.
  • A more visible Premium control beneath the header explains the one-time unlock. Purchase and restore messaging appears sooner, with clearer pending and connection states.
  • Backup, preview, format, verification and receipt guidance is easier to find. Multi-partition layout previews remain non-destructive until physical interoperability can be validated.

Availability on Play does not prove a completed customer purchase or physical USB compatibility.

October 7, 2026 · Version 2.4.2 (182) · SUPERSEDED

Full-featured formatting, easier discovery and a polished interface

This historical update included and superseded the unpublished 2.4.1 candidate. It was later superseded by 2.5.0 and 2.5.1.

  • A formatting-first dashboard: ten shipping writable filesystem options, Open drive formatter, and Explore all tools with 19 searchable tools and help topics.
  • The original larger, centered duck, cleaner title-to-artwork transition, live side lighting, consistent light/dark themes, and better small-window and large-text layouts.
  • A full-width Get Premium control below the compact header and a benefits dialog explaining the permanent purchase, free tools, restoration, and compatibility limits.
  • Stronger protection against duplicate checkout, clearer pending/canceled/error states, restore controls, and retry handling for payment acknowledgment.
  • A correction to recognition of official Google Play signing certificates, plus preservation of billing state when Android recreates the screen.
  • Prior prepared upgrades: searchable help, free local file/folder copying with duplicate choices, non-journaled HFS+ formatting (not APFS), allocation controls, and stronger backup, restore, and write-verification checks.
  • Updated native compression libraries, stronger operation safety checks, and offline software notices. Experimental formatter warnings and destructive-operation confirmations remain.

Final Google Play checkout, purchase activation, and restoration testing remain outstanding. See purchase help for current and upcoming controls.

Current access clarification · October 7, 2026

Free tools and one-time Premium

The current free-format allowance is compatible FAT12/FAT16 quick formatting of media up to 2 GB, alongside inspection, guidance, and previews. Older website text incorrectly promised one Smart Format per installation. Modern formatting and advanced drive operations require Premium.

August 26, 2026 · Version 2.2.7

Clearer navigation and safer USB decisions

  • Keeps Dashboard, Guide, Settings, IP, and About as visible primary sections rather than hiding them behind a scrolling tab strip.
  • Restores the DucyDrive duck header and its contained circuit-style data backdrop without changing navigation or tool behavior.
  • Clarifies the free download and permanent Premium unlock: no ads, subscriptions, coins, tokens, credits, or recurring charges.
  • Improves pre-write image and boot-media safety checks so the app can explain a compatibility concern before a selected USB drive is changed.
  • Includes guided formatting, image inspection, image writing, backup, restore, testing, receipts, and novice help where supported by the connected device.
  • Keeps user-initiated USB content processing local to the connected Android device.

The previous planned one-use Smart Format allowance is superseded by the current permanent small-media free-format boundary described above.

July 30, 2026

What the 2.2 program adds

  • Automatic ISO, IMG, raw DMG, Windows, Linux, rescue, and compressed-image inspection.
  • FAT32 split-WIM Windows media construction and transactional NTFS/ESP media creation.
  • Source hashing, byte read-back, partition, filesystem, and EFI loader-path validation.
  • Streaming gzip, ZIP, XZ, Bzip2, and Zstandard image support without a full temporary decompressed copy.
  • Detailed receipts that distinguish structural checks from QEMU or physical-firmware proof.

July 27, 2026

Version 2.0.1

  • Streams gzip-compressed raw images directly to USB.
  • Adds optional expected SHA-256 matching and read-back verification control.
  • Adds valid-GPT relocation and stale-metadata cleanup controls.
  • Expands the in-app User Manual across every major workflow.
  • Updates public support and privacy contact information.

July 27, 2026

Version 2.0.0

  • Introduces verified byte-for-byte IMG, ISO, and raw DMG restoration.
  • Reports detected ISO 9660, El Torito, MBR, GPT, and EFI structures.
  • Invalidates obsolete recovery snapshots before raw image writes.
  • Restricts USB transport to the supported Bulk-Only protocol.

Release policy

Shipped release history and unreleased preparation are labeled separately. A successful build or simulated payment-state test is not proof of a completed Google Play transaction. Publication is confirmed before an update is announced as available.
