# MJD Solutions — DucyDrive Legal & Support

Official public product, privacy, release, support, press, and platform-verification
information for DucyDrive AIO USB Tools. The site describes only the public,
user-facing Android experience and does not publish unverified boot claims or
private implementation material.

This repository intentionally contains no application source code, binaries,
signing material, license authority, customer information, or internal
documentation.

The Google Play edition is free to download. Inspection, guidance, previews,
and compatible FAT12/FAT16 quick formatting of media up to 2 GB are free.
Modern formatting and advanced drive operations require a one-time Google
Play Premium purchase. It does not use advertisements, subscriptions, coins,
tokens, or recurring charges.

Purchase and restoration help is on [the support page](support.html#payments).
Google controls available payment methods and displays the localized checkout
price. Use the purchasing Google account to restore access; an unresolved or
pending purchase is not a reason to pay again.

Version 2.5.10 (193) is the latest confirmed customer-available Google Play build.
It explains Android's folder-access step and offers an individual-file route for
Downloads. Version 2.5.11 (194), which opens the app's own File Explorer before
requesting folder access, has been submitted for review but is not yet live.
See [release notes](changelog.html).

[Interface screenshots](index.html#screenshots) are original virtual-handset
captures of submitted version 2.5.11, clearly labeled as a preview; they are
not proof of a real purchase or physical USB test. Historical real-device video
is labeled separately.
The public site states actual availability, while distinguishing emulator
screenshots from a verified customer purchase or physical USB test.
