# DucyDrive — official product, legal and support site

DucyDrive AIO USB Tools is an Android USB formatting and drive toolkit by MJD
Solutions. This repository publishes the product website, guides, privacy,
terms, support, release history and platform-verification information.

**Free to download does not mean every tool is free. Most USB formatting and
advanced drive operations require the paid, one-time Premium unlock.**

## Free versus Premium

| Free tools | Paid Premium tools |
| --- | --- |
| File Explorer: browse, search, preview and copy files where Android permits access | Other supported formatting setups, including FAT32, exFAT and NTFS |
| USB inspection and GPT analysis | Supported raw-image writing and drive-image backups |
| Tool guides and simulated setup previews | Drive speed and full-capacity testing, wiping, eligible GPT repair and verified metadata rollback |
| Limited legacy formatting: compatible drives up to 2 GiB (2,147,483,648 bytes), FAT12/FAT16, MBR layout, Quick mode and Data use only | Experimental formats retain their compatibility warnings |

The limited free-format allowance is permanent, not a one-use trial. FAT32,
exFAT and NTFS formatting require Premium even on small drives. Metadata
rollback is not recovery of deleted or overwritten files. Premium does not
bypass Android's protected system folders or other apps' private storage.
APFS formatting, Time Machine setup and universal boot compatibility are not
included. Always keep an independent backup before changing a drive.

## Purchase and regional pricing

DucyDrive has no ads, subscriptions, coins, credits, token packs or recurring
charges. A single Google Play purchase permanently unlocks the current Premium
tools for the purchasing Google account. Internet and Google Play are needed
to verify protected operations.

The app's price sticker and Premium explanation display the current product
price supplied by Google Play for the user's Play account country and available
currency. The app does not manually convert currencies or guess a missing price.
Review Google's final checkout price, payment methods and any applicable tax
before paying. The website does not advertise one fixed worldwide price.

[Get DucyDrive on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.michaelducylowycz.duchydriveaiousbtools),
then choose **Get Premium** in the app. The Dashboard paid-tools section provides
another purchase entry; it explains access before the separate Google Play
checkout action. [Compare access on the website](index.html#purchase).

Already paid or payment pending? Use the purchasing Google account and check or
restore access; do not buy again. See [purchase and restoration help](support.html#payments).
